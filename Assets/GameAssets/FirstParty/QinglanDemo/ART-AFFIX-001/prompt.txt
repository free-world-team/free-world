First-party deterministic construction specification for four elite-affix overlays used by all Qinglan Demo enemies.

Create four transparent square overlays without an enemy body, environment, UI frame, text, logo, watermark, or
third-party input. The center must remain open so the enemy silhouette and attack windup remain readable. Keep all
visible pixels safely inside the canvas. Glow is subordinate to opaque geometry. Each mechanic must remain distinct
in grayscale and common color-vision modes through its silhouette, marker shape, and spatial rhythm rather than hue.

Frenzy: three continuous tangent speed lanes and six directional chevrons. Use warm orange-gold, cream highlights,
and a dark earthen outline. The overlay communicates sustained movement and attack cadence without drawing a hazard
area through the enemy body.

Barrier: six separated shield facets forming a hexagonal shell, alternating facet values, plus six diamond anchors.
Use warm mineral gold and ivory, not player teal. The gaps clearly distinguish the shell from an area-of-effect ring.

Splitting: two broken rings, three outward crack marks, paired lower child diamonds, and two outward chevrons. Use a
muted rose/mineral palette with a dark outline. Do not depict more than one split generation and do not cover center.

Quake: three vertically compressed broken ground rings and eight radial triangular warning wedges. Use approved P0
danger orange-red with pale warning highlights and a deep earthen outline. Preserve the transparent interior and make
the ground-plane ellipse visually different from the other upright overlays.

Generate one deterministic 1024x1024 RGBA source PNG per affix, then Lanczos-downsample each to one 512x512 RGBA
runtime PNG. MipMaps remain off. Only runtime final files may enter QinglanDemo-Visual Addressables.
